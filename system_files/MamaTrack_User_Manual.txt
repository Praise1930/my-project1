1. ABOUT THE SYSTEM
===============================================================================
MamaTrack GPS makes an expectant mother's exact location visible to responders
before she reaches a health facility. It connects five kinds of users on one
shared record:

  * Expectant Mother  – raises an SOS, tracks the ambulance, follows her ANC
                        schedule and health ledger.
  * VHT Worker        – registers mothers, logs home visits, follows up
                        mothers who miss ANC contacts.
  * Doctor            – receives inbound patients, conducts ANC visits,
                        requests blood, completes handovers and audits.
  * Ambulance Driver  – receives dispatches, navigates to the patient and
                        the facility, logs fuel and pre-duty inspections.
  * Administrator     – triages and dispatches SOS alerts, monitors the
                        fleet, manages facilities, staff, mothers, blood
                        requests and reports.

Changes made on one device reach the other devices automatically (live sync).

2. BEFORE YOU START
===============================================================================
Requirements
  * A smartphone, tablet or computer with a modern browser
    (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
  * Location (GPS) permission switched ON — essential for mothers and drivers.
  * Internet connection for sync. The app keeps working with no network and
    catches up when the connection returns (see Section 11).

Installing as an app (recommended on phones)
  1. Open the web address above in Chrome.
  2. Tap the "Install" banner, or open the browser menu and choose
     "Add to Home screen" / "Install app".
  3. Open MamaTrack from the home-screen icon. It runs full-screen like a
     normal app.

Allow location
  When the browser asks "Allow MamaTrack to use your location?", choose
  Allow. If you refused by mistake, open the browser's site settings,
  set Location to Allow, and reload the page.

Theme
  Light and dark themes are supported; use the theme switch in the app.

3. SIGNING IN, REGISTERING AND RESETTING A PASSWORD
===============================================================================
3.1 Sign in
  1. Open the app. On the landing page choose your portal, or go to Login.
  2. At the top of the login card select your role:
        Mother | VHT | Doctor | Driver | Admin
     The role MUST match your account. Choosing the wrong role gives
     "User account not found with selected role".
  3. Enter your email address and password.
  4. Press Sign in. You are taken to your role's dashboard.

3.2 Register as a new expectant mother
  1. On the login page press "Register Mother Account".
  2. Fill in: full name, email, phone number, password (minimum 6
     characters) and confirm password.
  3. Fill in pregnancy start date (month/year), sub-county (Mukono
     Municipality, Goma, Nama, Koome, Ntenjeru), village/ward, and next of
     kin (name, phone, relationship).
  4. Submit. A confirmation email is sent — open it and click the link to
     verify, then sign in. (Check Spam / Junk if it does not arrive.)
  5. Your full 8-contact WHO ANC schedule is created automatically.
  A VHT can also register a mother on her behalf (Section 5).

3.3 Forgot password
  1. On the login page choose "Forgot password".
  2. Enter your registered email and submit.
  3. Follow the link in the email to set a new password.

3.4 Sign out
  Press the Logout / Exit icon in the menu or top bar. Always sign out on a
  shared device.

4. EXPECTANT MOTHER PORTAL
===============================================================================
Open the menu (top-left icon on phones) to move between sections.

4.1 Overview (Home)
  * Pregnancy progress — weeks pregnant and progress to delivery.
  * "Your antenatal progress" — how many of the 8 WHO ANC contacts are done.
  * Next Appointment — your soonest booked visit.
  * Your Health Score and personalised pregnancy insights.
  * Alert Notifications — messages from your doctor, VHT and the system.
    Use "Mark all read" to clear them.

4.2 Emergency — RAISING AN SOS
  Use this for danger signs such as:
    - Vaginal bleeding or sudden warm fluid leakage
    - Severe, persistent headache or blurred vision
    - Severe, constant abdominal pain or cramping
    - High fever, severe chills, or fits/convulsions
    - Sudden swelling of the face, hands or ankles
  Steps
    1. Go to Emergency and press "Trigger SOS"
       ("Trigger Maternal Rescue Beacon" / "Tap to dispatch ambulance").
    2. Allow location if asked. Your GPS position is attached to the alert.
    3. The district coordinator is alerted at once and dispatches an
       ambulance.
    4. Watch the status change: Pending -> Dispatched -> En-Route ->
       Arrived. The screen shows the Assigned Driver, ambulance Plate No,
       and Ambulance ETA, with the ambulance moving on the map.
    5. Keep the app open and your phone charged. Stay where you are if safe.
  Cancel
    Press "Cancel SOS" only if help is no longer needed. Cancelling is
    possible only before the ambulance picks you up.
  In a life-threatening emergency, also call the facility hotline shown in
  the app.

4.3 WHO ANC Checklist / Checkup Schedules
  * Shows all 8 WHO antenatal contacts with due dates.
  * Completed contacts show what the clinician recorded (BP, weight,
    fundal height, fetal heart rate, haemoglobin, urine protein, tetanus,
    malaria prevention, mosquito net, advice) and who recorded it.
  * Missed contacts are shown in red — contact your VHT or clinic.

4.4 Vitals history / Health Ledger
  * Log your own readings: blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), glucose
    level, fetal kicks, temperature. Press "Save & Validate Vitals".
  * All readings from you, your VHT and your doctor appear here with
    "Recorded By" and "Recorded Date/Time".
  * "Village Health Team Visits" lists every home visit by your VHT.

4.5 Appointments & Consult
  * Upcoming appointments and reminders.
  * "Expert Doctor Consultation" — type symptoms or questions to your
    doctor.

4.6 Profile & Contacts
  * Update medical history / allergies, village, sub-county and next of kin.
  * Upload a profile photo.
  * See your assigned VHT and doctors.

5. VILLAGE HEALTH TEAM (VHT) PORTAL
===============================================================================
5.1 Dashboard
  Counters: Mothers Monitored, Home Visits Logged, Active SOS Alerts,
  plus a notifications bell.

5.2 Register Mother
  1. Open "Register Mother".
  2. Enter the mother's full name, email, phone, date of birth, blood group,
     pregnancy start date, village and sub-county, and next of kin.
  3. Tick known risk factors (Previous C-Section, Chronic Hypertension,
     Gestational Diabetes, History of Haemorrhage, Multiple Pregnancy,
     Severe Anaemia Hb < 7).
  4. Save. You are recorded as her VHT and her 8-contact ANC schedule is
     created.

5.3 Assigned Mothers
  Search by name, village or phone. Open a mother to see her details.
  You can raise an SOS on a mother's behalf if she cannot.

5.4 Visit Logs — recording a home visit
  1. Choose the mother and the date.
  2. Enter Blood Pressure (e.g. 120/80), temperature (e.g. 36.6),
     fetal kicks and Fetal Movements Status
     (Normal / Active, Reduced / Sluggish, No movement felt).
  3. Select Observed Complications if any (e.g. Swollen Feet / Face,
     Premature Water Breaking) and write advice given to the mother.
  4. Press "Save Visit Log".
  Result: the mother is notified; if complications were recorded, every
  doctor is alerted.

5.5 ANC Follow-up
  * Every mother against her 8-contact schedule; defaulters (missed
    contacts) appear first with a red badge count.
  * "Send reminder" — sends an in-app notification and SMS to the mother.
  * "Book catch-up visit" — books a new date for the missed contact.

6. DOCTOR / CLINICIAN PORTAL
===============================================================================
6.1 Overview
  Duty Status (on/off duty), available ward beds, pending blood orders,
  and incoming alerts.

6.2 Inbound Transfers (emergencies)
  * Each inbound patient shows name, blood group, condition, ETA and
    distress notes, so the team can prepare before arrival.
  * CDSS triage (Uganda Clinical Guidelines) helps classify severity.
  * "View Official Uganda MoH Referral Note" opens the referral form.
  * Request blood: choose Requested Blood Group and units -> submit.
    The administrator approves and delivers it (Section 8.6); you are
    notified at each step.
  * Complete Handover: after arrival, enter Clinical Diagnosis / Findings,
    Treatment Plan Administered (e.g. C-section, IV drip) and the Handoff
    Outcome, then press "Complete Handover". This closes the case.
  * If the outcome is Deceased / Fatal complication, complete the MPDSR
    (Three-Delay Model) audit.

6.3 Antenatal Care Clinic
  1. Select a patient. Her progress bar shows WHO ANC contacts completed.
  2. Review the contact list, past results and her VHT home visits.
  3. "Conduct visit" opens the Uganda MoH ANC form. Record:
       BP (e.g. 120/80), weight, fundal height, fetal heart rate,
       haemoglobin, urine protein, temperature, tetanus toxoid,
       IPTp1–IPTp4, iron/folic acid, mosquito net (LLIN), HIV test,
       syphilis test, danger signs present, clinical findings and a
       note/advice for the mother.
  4. Press "Record visit".
  Result: the mother's checklist and vitals update; she receives an
  in-app message and SMS; abnormal results (high BP, low Hb, abnormal fetal
  heart rate, proteinuria, fever, danger signs) alert all doctors and her
  next of kin; the next contact is booked automatically.
  Also: "Book appointment" and "Send reminder".

6.4 My Treated Cases
  History of cases you handled, with trace and triage reports.

7. AMBULANCE DRIVER PORTAL
===============================================================================
7.1 Duty status
  Switch between ACTIVE ON-DUTY and STANDBY MODE. Only on-duty drivers
  receive dispatches. Keep location ON.

7.2 Pre-duty Safety Inspection (do this at the start of every shift)
  Tick: tyre pressure & suspension OK; engine fluid, oil & coolant OK;
  siren & warning lights working; obstetric delivery kit & oxygen tanks
  verified. Select Inspected Fuel Level (Full / Half / Low Tank) and submit
  ("Inspection Log Certified").

7.3 Responding to a dispatch (Active Mission)
  1. A new mission shows PICKUP (patient name and GPS), DESTINATION
     facility and ETA.
  2. Press "Start Trip & GPS Simulation" — status becomes En-Route and
     your position is shared with the mother, coordinator and hospital.
  3. On reaching the mother press "Arrived at Patient & Picked Up".
  4. Press "Start transit to hospital".
  5. On arrival press "Arrived at Hospital". The doctor then completes the
     handover and the ambulance is released.
  Steps only move forward; a finished case cannot be re-opened.

7.4 Fuel log
  Record fuel purchases: filling station name, amount and cost.

8. DISTRICT ADMINISTRATOR PORTAL
===============================================================================
8.1 GPS Dispatch (command console)
  * "INCOMING SOS ALERT" pops up with a sound for every new SOS.
  * Pending Dispatch Triage lists new alerts: case code, patient, distress
    notes and location on the map.
  * Dispatch: choose the Target Hospital Facility and Ambulance & Driver
    (the nearest capable facility is suggested), then press "Dispatch".
  * Active Dispatched Missions shows live ambulance positions.
  * Close out a case once it is Delivered (or force-close a case that ended
    off-system). This releases the ambulance.
  * Cancel a case only before pickup.
  * The bell shows Alert history.

8.2 Expectant Mothers
  Registered Expectant Mothers Directory with ANC Progress column. Add,
  edit or deactivate mothers; view next of kin and due dates.

8.3 Duty Personnel
  Obstetricians & Medical Specialists Roster and Ambulance Drivers Roster.
  Add personnel (name, email, phone, role, licence number, specialisation,
  assigned hospital/vehicle, shift) and set a new account password.
  Press "Save Personnel".

8.4 Health Facilities
  Hospital Referral Network Directory. Add or edit a facility: name, type
  (Government / Private / NGO-Mission), level, address, hotline, email,
  available ward beds, CEmONC capability, blood bank, surgical theatre.
  Keep bed counts current — dispatch uses them. Press "Save Facility".

8.5 Reports & Audits
  Historical rescue referral audit logs, Audit & Fuel Logs, pre-duty
  inspections, DHIS2 export.

8.6 Blood Bank Requests
  Requests raised by doctors appear here. Press Approve or Decline, then
  "Mark delivered" once issued. The requesting doctor is notified by app
  and SMS.

8.7 MPDSR Surveillance
  Maternal and perinatal death reviews: audit status and corrective action
  plans.

8.8 System Performance
  Uptime, average dispatch latency, GPS sync accuracy and traffic volume.

9. HOW AN EMERGENCY MOVES THROUGH THE SYSTEM
===============================================================================
  Mother / VHT          Administrator         Driver               Doctor
  ------------          -------------         ------               ------
  Trigger SOS  ------>  INCOMING SOS
                        Dispatch     ------>  New mission
  sees Dispatched                             Start trip  (en_route)
  sees ETA / map                              Picked up   (arrived)
                                              Start transit (in_transit)  -> Inbound
                                              Arrived at hospital (delivered)
                        Close out                                  Complete Handover
                                                                  (completed)

  Status order: pending -> dispatched -> en_route -> arrived ->
                in_transit -> delivered -> completed
  (or cancelled, before pickup only)

===============================================================================
10. HOW ROUTINE ANTENATAL CARE MOVES THROUGH THE SYSTEM
===============================================================================
  1. Mother registers (herself or via VHT) -> 8 ANC contacts are scheduled
     from her pregnancy start date.
  2. VHT logs home visits -> mother and (if complications) doctors notified.
  3. Doctor conducts each ANC visit -> results saved, mother notified,
     abnormal findings escalated, next contact booked.
  4. A contact not done within 14 days of its date is marked missed ->
     mother sees it in red; VHT sees her in ANC Follow-up and sends a
     reminder or books a catch-up visit.
  5. Administrator monitors ANC progress for every mother.

===============================================================================
11. WORKING OFFLINE
===============================================================================
  * With no network the app keeps working; your actions are saved on the
    device and queued.
  * When the connection returns they are sent automatically. Do not clear
    browser data while offline or queued changes will be lost.
  * An SOS raised offline is sent as soon as any signal is available — in
    a real emergency also phone the facility hotline.

12. TROUBLESHOOTING
===============================================================================
  Problem                                   What to do
  ----------------------------------------  ---------------------------------
  "User account not found with selected     Select the correct role tab
   role"                                    (Mother/VHT/Doctor/Driver/Admin).
  "Incorrect credentials"                   Re-type the password (case
                                            sensitive) or use Forgot password.
  "Email address has not been verified"     Open the confirmation email
                                            (check Spam/Junk) or resend it.
  "Account is deactivated"                  Contact the administrator.
  Map shows wrong place / no location       Turn on GPS and allow location
                                            for the site, then reload.
  Data not updating on another device       Check internet; wait a few
                                            seconds; pull to refresh / reload.
  Installed icon opens in browser           Remove it and re-install from
                                            Chrome's "Install app".
  Blank or error screen                     Reload the page; the error
                                            screen resets when you navigate.

13. SAFETY AND DATA NOTICE
===============================================================================
  * This deployment contains demonstration (fictional) data and shared
    demo passwords. It must NOT hold real patient data until database
    access is restricted per user and accounts are moved to managed
    authentication.
  * Never share your password. Sign out on shared devices.
  * MamaTrack supports, and does not replace, clinical judgement and
    standard emergency telephone procedures.
